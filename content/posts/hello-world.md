Every developer's journey starts with a hello world. This blog is mine.

## Why this site exists

I'm a DevOps engineer. I spend my days (and a fair amount of nights) working
with Linux systems, Docker containers, virtualized infrastructure, and the
networks that glue them together. I've been running my own home lab for a
while now — virtual hosts, self-hosted services, automation scripts — and
most of what I know came from breaking things and fixing them.

This site is my lab notebook, made public. I want a place to:

- write down what I build and how I built it
- document problems I solved so I (and you) don't have to solve them twice
- share notes on the tools and tech I'm actually using

## What you'll find here

Expect posts about infrastructure, Docker, virtualization, networking,
deployments, and the occasional rant about something that took me three hours
to debug (it'll probably be DNS).

The whole thing is self-hosted — Next.js behind nginx on my own server. The
source is public on GitHub, so even the site itself is an example of what I
write about.

## Why self-host?

Because it's the best way to learn. You don't really understand a stack until
you've installed it, configured it, broken it, and fixed it yourself. Also,
it's fun.

Thanks for reading. Check back — new posts are on the way.
